# 0.15.2

Marcus reads the project board, current team, staffing and resource approvals when reviewing a planning question. A sender can ask the question in prose and link the accepted production brief. Missing or ambiguous board context goes back to the team for clarification.

The Director assesses the selected action against the accepted plan and vision. An owner request identifies the current requirement, the proposed change and its rationale. Routine coordination and capacity discovery stay with the project. Recovery recognizes older agent-generated escalations and preserves human answers.
