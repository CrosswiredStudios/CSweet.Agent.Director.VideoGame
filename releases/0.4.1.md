# 0.4.1

Recover the retained ready production brief when an older Producer stopped at the four-draft ceiling before structured correction receipts existed. Director attention verifies the exact current project, participants, accepted sources, latest brief and independent creative findings, journals one bounded retry, and resumes the existing collaboration. The resumed Director callback relays the actual candidate-bound corrections instead of repeating the historical blocker.

Pending or paused owner decisions, stale/foreign evidence, cancelled sessions and already bounded corrective failures are never automatically resumed. Lost resume responses are recovered through current-state reads and stable idempotency. No scope, staffing, spending or release approval is manufactured.

Requires Producer 6.2.0 and Creative Director 4.1.0. Publish and update through Installed Agents; no stored-business or installed-payload edits are required.
