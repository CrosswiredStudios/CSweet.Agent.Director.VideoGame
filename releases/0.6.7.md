# 0.6.7

When Naomi's creative review of the production brief uses up its three attempts, Marcus now raises an owner
decision instead of leaving the review blocked with no way forward.

- **The decision** (`video-game.creative-review-recovery.v1`, authority `material-strategy-change`, shown to the owner
  as a project decision card) states the last rejection reason when one is known and offers:
  - **Retry the creative review** (recommended): Naomi reviews the same brief again with a fresh round of three attempts.
  - **Ask Gabriel to revise the brief**: Marcus relays the decision and reason to Gabriel, who submits a new revision;
    it gets a new creative review.
  - **Keep it paused**: planning and staffing stay blocked until a later choice.
- **Fresh rounds.** Failures are counted from the owner's latest choice, so each retry gets three new attempts. Marcus's
  automatic recovery still stops after three failures in a round and never retries on its own after that.
- **Acting on the choice.** A decided retry resumes the review session; a decided revision resumes the brief collaboration
  so Marcus can ask Gabriel once. Attention review repeats the same check if the decision event was missed.
- Each exhausted round raises at most one decision (idempotent per round).

Why: on 2026-11-11 QFN Games' production brief stayed blocked after Creative Director 4.1.0 rejected three reviews. Once
4.1.2 fixed the cause, the review still could not recover: Marcus's three-attempt limit was used up, his automatic
recovery stopped, and the owner's Retry button returned the turn to Marcus, who blocked again.

A review already blocked before this version (such as QFN Games') needs no action: on Marcus's next attention review
he resumes it once, which raises this decision. Choose "Retry the creative review".
